01 / The big picture

A vision built on three pillars.

Saudi Vision 2030 is a national transformation framework. Its ambitions depend on the choices and contributions of people.

💗Vibrant Society

Culture, wellbeing and connected communities.

💡Thriving Economy

Skills, opportunity and entrepreneurship.

🚀Ambitious Nation

Responsibility, effective institutions and ambition.
